Cross-Segment Verdict
Where the best wedge lives · recommended sequencing
Best near-term wedge
GLP-1 Weight Loss
Massive demand (1.77M SV), softest review wall (0% rev behind 5k+), 95% of revenue from 2025+ launches. The wall isn't built yet.
Largest scale opportunity
Menopause Support
$233M TAM, $87/search monetization, premium tolerated to $80+. But review-locked: 73% of revenue behind 5k+ wall. A safety/transparency play has the cleanest opening.
Sequencing recommendation
Lead GLP-1, then Support
Enter GLP-1 with honest positioning to win share before the moat hardens. Build review momentum, then expand into Support with proven trust signals. Skip MWL — Estroven-locked, low rev/search.
READ-THROUGH   The dominant signal across all three segments is that incumbent trust is breaking faster than incumbent share. GLP-1 is structurally open right now; Menopause Support has a credibility gap a transparent operator can walk through.
